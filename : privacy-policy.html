Privacy Policy for SmartShop POS

Last updated: October 11, 2026

SmartShop POS ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SmartShop POS mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide core functionality such as user authentication and data synchronization, our App collects minimal necessary information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address and profile details to identify your account and secure your business data.
  • Business & Transaction Data: Information entered into the App regarding your sales, inventory, customer records, and credits is stored securely via Google Cloud Firestore to maintain your POS records.
